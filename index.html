FAQ

Good to know

Quick answers to what customers ask most. Anything else, message us on WhatsApp.

  • What do I need to rent a car?

    A valid driving licence and your passport or Emirates ID. Visitors may also need an International Driving Permit, depending on the country that issued their licence. Send us a photo of your documents on WhatsApp and we will confirm before you book.

  • Is there a security deposit?

    Yes. A refundable security deposit is taken for every rental. The amount depends on the car and the rental length, and we tell you the exact figure with your quote, before anything is booked.

  • Is there a minimum age?

    Yes. The minimum age and driving experience depend on the car. Tell us your age when you enquire and we will confirm which cars are available to you.

  • Can you deliver the car?

    Ask us about delivery and collection to your hotel, home or the airport. We will confirm the options and any charge for your location with your quote.

  • How do I pay?

    We accept PayPal, Mastercard, Visa, American Express and Bitcoin. Payment is only taken once the car, dates and price have been confirmed with you.

  • How do I book?

    Message us on WhatsApp or call +971 56 218 2999 with the car and your dates. We confirm availability and the rate, then arrange the documents and handover.
