About Elite Fleet Lease On

An independent leasing house in Dubai. We lease luxury cars to individuals and companies, monthly or for up to 12 months.

The Elite Fleet difference

  • Fewer promises, kept.

    Every listing shows only what is verified about the car. If a detail is missing, we say so rather than fill the gap.

  • One contact, start to finish.

    From the first message to the end of your lease, you speak with the same team.

  • Quotes confirmed, never assumed.

    Availability, the monthly price and the deposit are confirmed with you personally before anything is signed.

Good to know

Quick answers to what customers ask most. Anything else, message us on WhatsApp.

  • What do I need to lease a car?

    Individuals: a valid driving licence and your passport or Emirates ID. Companies: a valid trade licence and the authorised signatory’s ID. The minimum age depends on the car. Send us your documents on WhatsApp and we will confirm before anything is signed.

  • How long can I lease for?

    From one month up to 12 months. Tell us the term you need and we will send a quote for it.

  • Is there a security deposit?

    Yes. A refundable security deposit is taken for every lease. The amount depends on the car and the lease term, and we tell you the exact figure with your quote, before anything is signed.

  • Can you deliver the car?

    Ask us about delivery and collection to your home, office or hotel. We will confirm the options and any charge for your location with your quote.

  • How do I pay?

    We accept PayPal, Mastercard, Visa, American Express and Bitcoin. Payment is only taken once the car, lease term and price have been confirmed with you.

  • How do I get a quote?

    Message us on WhatsApp or call +971 56 218 2999 with the car and the lease term you need. We confirm availability and send your quote, then arrange the documents and handover.
